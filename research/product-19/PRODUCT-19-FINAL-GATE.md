## 9. Cannibalization, risk, cost

- **Cannibalization: LOW.** Same job as #1–#7, but a separate acquisition door (French search, French title and tags) and a separate buyer (French-speaking households). An English buyer will not choose it over #1.
- **Legal: LOW.** Budgeting only; no tax, credit or investment advice.
- **Privacy: LOW–MEDIUM.** Household amounts stay in the buyer's own copy.
- **Support: MEDIUM.** French-language questions. Mitigations: a French Quick Start PDF, French in-sheet instructions, and short pre-written French support replies.
- **Misunderstanding risk: LOW–MEDIUM.** The decimal comma, € formatting and dd/mm/yyyy dates must work in both Sheets and Excel (see handoff).
- **Build tier: 3 (LIGHT) to low 2.** Reuses the factory budget architecture; 6 visible tabs.
