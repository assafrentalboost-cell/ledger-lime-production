# CLAUDE BUILD HANDOFF

## A. Product

- **Name:** Home Renovation Planner & Budget Tracker
- **Buyer:** homeowner planning or running a renovation or remodel (one home, one or more rooms).
- **Promise:** "Know what every room will cost, pick the right quote, and see the moment you go over budget."
- **Price:** ₪69 (≈ $19). No discount without Assaf's approval.
- **Platforms:** Google Sheets master (Make-a-Copy link) + Excel .xlsx, per house standard. Excel = Sheets on all Dashboard values.

## B. Tabs (maximum 7 visible + hidden Lists)

1. **Start Here** — 3 setup steps, currency, project name, total budget, contingency % (default 10%).
2. **Rooms & Budget** — up to 15 rooms/areas. Per room: planned budget, committed (accepted quotes), spent (actuals), remaining, status.
3. **Quotes** — up to 3 quotes per line item (contractor/vendor, amount, notes). The buyer marks one as Selected; the selected amount flows to Committed for that room.
4. **Expenses** — date, room, category (Labor, Materials, Fixtures, Permits, Design, Other), vendor, amount, paid/unpaid.
5. **Payment Schedule** — per vendor: deposit and milestone payments with due dates; status Upcoming / Due soon (≤7 days) / Overdue / Paid.
6. **Dashboard** — total budget vs committed vs spent; contingency used / remaining; budget-vs-actual bar per room; top 3 rooms over budget; next 5 payments due.
7. **Shopping & Selections** — item, room, link, price, quantity, ordered/delivered status. Totals feed the room budget.
- **Lists** (hidden) — rooms, categories, statuses.

## C. Status logic (exact)

Per room:
- **On track:** spent ≤ 90% of planned and committed ≤ planned.
- **Watch:** spent > 90% of planned, or committed > planned.
- **Over budget:** spent > planned.
- **Not started:** no committed and no spent.

Project level:
- Contingency used = max(0, Σ spent − Σ planned).
- Contingency remaining = contingency amount − contingency used.
- When contingency remaining < 0, the Dashboard banner turns red: "Over total budget by X".

Payments: Overdue = unpaid and due date < today; Due soon = unpaid and due within 7 days.

## D. Sample data

One realistic project: kitchen + main bathroom + flooring. Total budget $48,000, 10% contingency. Two quotes per major item. At least one room in Watch and one Over budget, so the hero and video have a real state to show. All Dashboard figures must come from the real workbook recalculation, never typed into images.

## E. Explicit exclusions

- No change-order module, job costing, WIP or contractor-side margin (the rejected wedge).
- No loan, mortgage or financing calculators.
- No tax, permit-law or insurance guidance.
- No macros; no password-locked formulas (penalized in reviews). Use warning-only protection on formula columns.
- No Gantt chart in V1 (keeps it out of "overkill").

## F. Safe claims

Allowed: "plan your renovation budget", "compare quotes", "see over-budget rooms instantly", "track payments to contractors".
Not allowed: guaranteed savings, "contractor-proof", legal/insurance claims.

## G. QA requirements

- Formula QA on every status branch in §C, including boundary values (exactly 90%, exactly 100%, zero budget, blank rows).
- Selected-quote switching updates Committed and the Dashboard.
- Year-independent dates (no hard-coded year).
- Make-a-Copy test in Sheets; protection survives; 0 errors.
- Excel = Sheets on all Dashboard values.
- Sheets visual pass: no column headers breaking mid-word (lesson from P16).
- Print test: Dashboard and Rooms & Budget fit on one page each.
