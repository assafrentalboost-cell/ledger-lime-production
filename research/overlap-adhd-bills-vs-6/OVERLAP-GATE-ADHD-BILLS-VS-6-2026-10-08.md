## Decision

**REFRAME #6. No new SKU.**

- A bills-only ADHD listing has no proof of demand: 30 ADHD-titled bill listings sold 9 units in total over 12 months.
- The ADHD money buyers who do buy are buying **budget + monthly bill tracker hybrids**.
- The generic "bill tracker" door already belongs to **#4 Between Paydays** (its tags include "bill calendar" and "bill tracker"). That door is a $2–5 market.
- So the bill-control job belongs **inside #6**, as the job the buyer sees first, not in a fourth money SKU.

The minimum of 3 is necessary, not sufficient, so **DISTINCT PRODUCT is not supported.**

## Step 8. Outcome: **B. PRODUCT #6 REFRAME / UPGRADE**

The bill-control job is real and demand-proven, but only as the **front door of an ADHD budget**. #6 has no traffic and is positioned on low-volume "calm, one page" words. Moving the bill job into #6's title, tags and hero follows the evidence. A new SKU would add a fourth money listing on a $2–5 door.
