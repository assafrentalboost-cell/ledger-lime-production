## 7. Contradictions

- **Entry door vs incumbent.** The biggest door ("clinical hours tracker") is 57% held by one mass catalog at $6.98, and that listing is *growing* (34 → 249/month). The premium thesis rests on smaller credential doors (≈400 units each).
- **Entrant proof sits beside the core SKU.** The strongest clean entrant sells an *IEP progress tracker* (SPED/BCBA data), not a licensure-hours tracker. Young licensure-hours entrants died.
- **Scale.** Specialists sustain 12/12, but at 30–190 units per SKU per year. The ceiling per product is modest.
- **New buyer, no inherited traffic.** Department #1's failures happened *with* adjacent catalog traffic. This audience starts from zero inside a household-finance-branded shop.
- **Rules accuracy is a liability.** BACB, state-board and CACREP rules change (2027). A wrong % rule means refunds and bad reviews.

## 8. Reasons the winner may still be wrong

1. Official Insights may show tiny volumes for "bcba fieldwork tracker", "lpc hours tracker" and "supervision log" (EverBee: CEU ≈5).
2. Free templates (spreadsheetdaddy, Paperform) and SaaS trackers (Ripley, MentraTrack, FieldworkABA, Track Your Hours) may cap paid demand. The SaaS tools already generate M-FVFs.
3. Supervisors or agencies often hand out tracking tools, which shrinks the trainee buyer pool.
4. The 2027 transition demand may be a one-time spike that fades after Jan 2027.
5. The mass-shop clone wave (ExclusiveDesignLab, then ExclusiveHouse) could reach the credential doors within months: ExclusiveDesignLab already listed a $33.98 BCBA fieldwork tracker 4 months ago.
6. Brand mismatch: professional buyers may not trust a household-finance shop. A separate shop or section might be needed.
7. Notary had *cleaner* entrant proof and lower flood. A red-team weighting that favors safety over depth flips the order.
8. PHI exposure: caseload, authorization and notes products tempt buyers to enter client data. Support and compliance risk.
9. The buyer-language pass used blogs and vendor pages, not Etsy reviews or Reddit threads.
10. Volume per SKU (30–190 units/year) may not cover L&L's build + QA + creative cost per product.
