| Maker pricing | "Recipe cost", "profit margin", "Etsy fees" | Etsy titles | Pass (generic) |

**The time-bound driver in the winner.** The BACB 2027 fieldwork rules take effect for applications from Jan 1, 2027:
- Concentrated-fieldwork supervision drops from 10% to 7.5%.
- There is a 160-hour monthly cap.
- Observation becomes duration-based.
- Trainees are advised to complete *both* the 2022 and 2027 monthly verification forms each month until they know which rule set they will apply under.

That is a concrete reconcile-two-rule-sets job with a deadline. Sources: [BACB 2027 transition](https://www.bacb.com/BCBA-2027-transition-resource), [Ripley](https://ripleyfieldworktracker.com/bacb-2027-requirements/), [MentraTrack](https://www.mentratrack.com/blog/bcba-fieldwork-tracker).
