## Phase 12 — Top 3 department candidates

### 1. Household Readiness & Family Records

- **Core buyer:** a household organizer (typically a parent, 28–55) who wants the family's operating information in one place: bills, accounts, documents, contacts, home, kids, pets, "if something happens".
- **Problem family:** "Nobody but me knows where anything is or what to do."
- **Cluster:** 17 terms, ≈3,400 searches/month (EverBee).
- **Estimated demand:** top-30 sales ≈17.7k units/12 months across the two sub-clusters (some overlap).
- **Competition:** 1–9k listings per term. Moderate concentration (38–41% top 3), with two cross-cluster incumbents.
- **Strongest 5 terms:** family command center, life binder, household binder, emergency binder, home management binder.
- **Competitor signals:**
  1. khdigitaldesignsco: 4,000+ units across siblings, $15–18.
  2. MyPlansCo: $37.48, 12/12, 493 reviews.
  3. CoyneManagement: Excel + PDF $39.99, single-product shop.
  4. ForYourVillageShop: two 12/12 siblings in a 24-month shop.
  5. PlanGoodLife / LinkDigitalStudio clone pair: a warning signal.
- **Clean entrant proof:** 4 clean shops (ToAllMyMemories, ElthivarDigitalCo, FamcarCreates, ForYourVillageShop).
- **Price durability:** median ≈$10; premium $28–40 proven, including a spreadsheet.
- **Trend:** mature/stable; some leaders declining.
- **Depth:** 8–10 distinct products (roadmap below).
- **Cross-sell:**
  - Within the department: emergency ↔ home mgmt ↔ babysitter ↔ pet ↔ inventory ↔ maintenance.
  - Into the existing catalog: #11 Aging Parent, #12 Estate Settlement, #16 Medical Bills, #18 Home Renovation.
- **Format diversity:** high.
- **Biggest risk:** the market is printable-led at about $10, and official conversion on "binder" terms may be Very low (canonical: "estate planning binder" 191 VL). L&L's spreadsheet products may draw views without sales, as caregiving did.
- **Missing evidence:** official Insights for 12 core terms; buyer reviews; seasonality; whether spreadsheet SKUs convert beyond CoyneManagement and APlusYouStore.

## Phase 13 — Provisional winner
