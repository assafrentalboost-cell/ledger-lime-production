**Product value mechanism (why someone might buy):** shared family money for a parent's care — reimbursement status, bills, contributions, a family handoff page. **Unproven**: zero sales, and category sales are at zero.

**Conclusion:** #11 shows that a buyer-identity title plus a clear conflict-resolving hero can win *views* in a thin search pocket. It does **not** show that the caregiving domain converts. Prior evidence says the opposite: across three passes the domain shows views without sales. **The decisive missing input is #11's Etsy Stats search-term breakdown** (which queries produce the views). ## 5. Wedge (if HOLD clears)

**Record → check completeness → hand off.**
- One inventory: accounts, bills/autopays, insurance, documents (with **location only, never passwords**), contacts, digital accounts, wishes.
- A **Readiness score**: required fields per item (owner, where to find it, who to contact, beneficiary/POD where relevant), with missing items flagged.
- A one-page **"If something happens to me — start here"** handoff, sorted into first 24 hours / first week / first month.
- An **autopay shutdown list**: recurring payments with "who must stop or transfer this".

Better than printable death binders: it is live and self-checking, and stays shareable as one file. Better than #12: it is the *owner's* tool before the event, while #12 is the executor's after it.

**Hero:** Readiness 64% → add a beneficiary to the life-insurance row → 71%, and the red "Missing beneficiary" flag clears.
**Video:** add a policy → three red flags (no location, no contact, no beneficiary) → fill them in → the score rises and the handoff page lists the policy.
