## 5. Canva licensing and publishing

Secondary sources plus Canva help-page snippets; canva.com itself was unreachable.

| Rule | Finding | Source quality |
|---|---|---|
| Templates containing **Pro** content | May be sold **only as Canva template links**, not exported files | Canva Help Center snippet + 3 secondary sources |
| Templates using **Free** content / own uploads | More flexibility. CLA §6 governs Free Content in templates for sale; the snippet was cut off. | Partial |
| Older Canva Zendesk text | Says Pro content cannot be in templates sold to third parties. It is stricter and about 4.5 years old, so treated as superseded but **not confirmed**. | Conflicting |
| Free-account publishing | Free users can publish to a **free my.canva.site address**, with **up to 5 live websites** (Pro: unlimited) | Canva help snippet |
| Free-account section limit | Free users can design up to 20 sections per page but **publish only up to 10 sections per page** | Canva help snippet |
| Custom domain | Requires a paid plan (Pro / Teams / Education) | Canva help snippet |
| Free-domain reuse | A previously used free domain cannot be reused | Canva help snippet |

**Design rules if built** (needs H3 confirmation):
- **Free elements and own uploads only, no Pro elements.** That way a free-account buyer sees no watermark and either delivery method stays legal.
- **10 sections or fewer per page**, so it publishes on a free account.
- Deliver as a **template link**.
- The buyer publishes to my.canva.site for free. A custom domain is optional and needs Pro.

## 6. Buyer-visible mechanism and price

**Mechanism: "The RSVP that counts itself."**
- The website RSVP feeds a matching Google Form, which fills a pre-built **Guest Count sheet**.
- The sheet shows Attending / Declined / Not yet replied, meal counts, dietary notes, plus-ones and song requests, and updates live.
- This uses L&L's spreadsheet strength, and it is one image in a hero: a phone showing the site, then the sheet tallying "Attending 86 · Chicken 41 · Fish 30 · Veg 15 · Not replied 22".
- **Kit contents:**
  - website (≤10 sections, free elements)
  - matching digital invitation and save-the-date (Canva, phone-sized)
  - QR card for printed invites
  - RSVP form + count sheet
  - illustrated setup guide
- **Uniqueness is unverified** (H4). If a top competitor already ships a results tally, the wedge drops to "design + better setup", which does not justify a design-led SKU from L&L.

**Price:**
- **List $32 (≈ ₪119 at an assumed 3.70 ILS/USD).**
- Under the 25% sale strategy that is **≈ $24 / ₪89 effective**. This is just below the $26.28 unit-weighted average and inside the 47% of units sold at $30–40 list.
- **Do not price at $45+.** Only Oct19CreativeStudio shows it working, with an established design.
